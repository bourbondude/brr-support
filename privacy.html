BRR Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

BRR is an independent relay companion app. It has no advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or data brokerage.

Information stored on your devices

Your selected legs, runner name, team code, display settings, and a random per-install identifier are stored on your device. If you choose to save a run, the app writes the workout, distance, and route to Apple Health. Health data is handled by HealthKit and is not uploaded to the app's CloudKit database.

Team information stored in CloudKit

Team features are optional. When a runner joins a team, the app stores their name, random per-install identifier, selected legs, team-size preference, and update time in Apple's CloudKit public database. While the runner explicitly has a leg in progress, the app also stores their precise location, leg number, progress, live pace, battery level, and report time. This data is used only to show teammates and spectators where the runner is and project exchange times.

Team codes are sharing credentials, not passwords. Anyone who has a team code and access to the web viewer can see that team's current runner information. Share a code only with teammates and intended spectators.

Retention and deletion

A live-location record is overwritten rather than retained as a location history, and the app requests its deletion when the leg finishes, sharing stops, or the runner leaves the team. The roster record is deleted when the runner leaves the team or switches to spectator mode. If the device is offline, CloudKit may not receive that deletion immediately; contact support to request removal of a remaining record.

Apple services and permissions

The app uses Apple CloudKit for optional team sharing, WeatherKit for exchange forecasts, HealthKit for optional workout recording, and Location Services for live progress. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy. You can revoke Location or Health access in system Settings and can use the course, maps, and elevation information without granting those permissions.
